A modern, high-performance, and fully responsive frontend web platform designed for **Apex Care International Hospital** — a premier JCI-accredited medical center. This platform highlights clinical excellence, emergency services, medical departments, doctor directories, wellness packages, and interactive patient appointment scheduling.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Highlights

- **🚨 24/7 Emergency & Operations Header:** Quick access to hotline numbers (`911`, `+1 800 555-APEX`), email contact, and real-time operational status.
- **🏥 Comprehensive Services & Departments:** Structured listings for Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Laboratory Diagnostics, and 24/7 Pharmacy.
- **👨‍⚕️ Specialist Doctors Directory:** Detailed profile cards featuring qualifications, availability schedules, and direct consultation options.
- **📅 Interactive Appointment Booking:** Patient-friendly booking form with department selection, preferred specialist choice, time slots, and HIPAA-compliant privacy notes.
- **💳 Preventive Healthcare Packages:** Transparent pricing cards for *Basic Wellness*, *Comprehensive Cardiac*, and *Executive Premium* health checkups.
- **💬 Patient Experience & FAQs:** Testimonials section for social proof and expandable FAQ section for common patient queries.
- **📍 Contact & Campus Information:** Integrated campus location details, working hours, and direct inquiry forms.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 / Custom Styling (Glassmorphism & Modern UI)
- **Scripting:** JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Icons & Typography:** Modern web icons & Google Fonts
- **Layout:** CSS Grid & Flexbox (Fully Responsive)
