Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request emergency ambulance dispatch?
Call our 24/7 dedicated dispatch emergency line at 911 or direct hospital extension +1 (800) 555-APEX immediately. Our GPS-monitored ambulances deploy within 3 minutes of confirmation.
Which insurance providers do you accept?
Apex Care partners with over 50 international and domestic healthcare insurers, including BlueCross, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare. Direct billing options are available.
Can I access my medical lab reports online?
Yes, all laboratory and radiology reports are available securely on our Patient Web Portal within 24 hours of diagnostic processing.
